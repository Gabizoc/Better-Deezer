🎧 Better Deezer redesigns the Deezer web player and adds dozens of small touches. Unofficial extension, not affiliated with Deezer.

🎨 THEMES
• 6 built-in themes, light, dark or following your system
• A Marketplace of community themes
• An editor with live preview and a readability check

🎤 NOW PLAYING
• Big cover and synced lyrics, word by word when Deezer has them

🪟 LAYOUT
• Centered search (Ctrl K), floating sidebar and player
• Album and artist headers tinted by the cover
• Restyled menus, notifications and dialogs

🎛️ COMFORT
• Volume: one click unfolds a slider, the mouse wheel adjusts it (5%, 1% with Shift)
• Mouse wheel on the player's cover to skip 5 s
• Home: hide sections, pick their order, "Don't recommend" list
• Sleep timer, compact mode, promotional banners hidden

🌍 In 7 languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch.

⚙️ Everything is set in the Better Deezer settings (Ctrl ,).

🔒 Privacy: no account, no analytics, no server. Your settings stay in your browser.

💜 Source code (MIT license): https://github.com/Gabizoc/Better-Deezer
